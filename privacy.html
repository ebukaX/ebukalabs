Privacy

Last updated: [14/11/2026]

The short version: this site doesn't track you. It's a static portfolio hosted on GitHub Pages, with no cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers and no third-party scripts. Fonts are served from this site, so your browser isn't contacting anyone else while you read.

The contact form

The form does not send anything to this website. It opens your own email app with the message filled in. If you choose to send it, it goes straight to my inbox. I use your email address and message only to reply to you. I won't share them or add you to any list, and I'll delete them if you ask.

Your light or dark choice

It is not saved anywhere. It resets when you reload the page.

Hosting

GitHub hosts this site and may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses. I can't see them. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Links to other sites

This site links to places like LinkedIn, GitHub and Substack. Once you click through, their privacy policies apply, not this one.

Changes

If I ever add something like analytics, I'll update this page and the date above first.
